# Audience AI Ad Prompt Pack

This optional pack helps viewers plan ads before using NK Studio or Higgsfield manually. NK Studio's separate B-roll Generator builds its own editable video prompt from one script line.

## Prompt 1 — turn a messy idea into the three answers the app needs

Paste this into ChatGPT, Codex or Claude:

```text
Act as my advertising strategist. Ask me, one question at a time, only for:
1. what I am selling;
2. who should buy it;
3. what painful problem or desired result matters most;
4. the offer or message;
5. the brand look;
6. anything that must remain accurate or must be avoided.

I may answer badly or briefly. Turn my answers into six short, concrete fields I can paste into an AI ad maker. Do not invent claims, prices, logos or product features. Then give me three distinct directions: Product Hero, Problem to Result, and Creator Style. Each direction must include one selling idea, one visual direction, one ready image-generation prompt and one restrained image-to-video motion prompt. If I provide a successful-ad screenshot, borrow only abstract mechanics such as hook, lighting, composition, camera feel, pacing and payoff. Never copy its brand, product, people, text or exact composition, and never promise virality.
```

## Prompt 2 — Product Hero image

```text
Create a premium [RATIO] product advertisement for [PRODUCT]. Use my uploaded product photo as the identity reference and preserve the real package, label placement, colors, proportions and materials. Make the product the dominant focal point and make [DESIRED RESULT] feel desirable for [BUYER]. Offer context: [OFFER]. Brand look: [BRAND LOOK]. Use intentional commercial composition, realistic material detail and clean space for editor-added copy. Do not invent logos, claims, competitor elements or decorative text inside the image.
```

## Prompt 3 — Problem-to-Result image

```text
Create a clear [RATIO] advertising image for [PRODUCT]. Show [BUYER]'s current friction at the frame edges while the exact product creates a calm, controlled center representing [DESIRED RESULT]. Use my uploaded product photo as the identity reference and preserve package, label, proportions and materials. Offer context: [OFFER]. Brand look: [BRAND LOOK]. Keep one dominant focal point and immediate mobile readability. Do not invent logos, claims, competitor elements or text inside the image.
```

## Prompt 4 — Creator Style image

```text
Create a believable creator-style [RATIO] advertisement for [PRODUCT]. Show a realistic person from the [BUYER] audience using the exact product in a natural routine where [DESIRED RESULT] is visible. Preserve the real package, label, colors, proportions and materials from my uploaded product photo. Keep the product and benefit immediately clear with premium but natural light and no influencer exaggeration. Offer context: [OFFER]. Do not invent logos, claims, competitor elements or text inside the image.
```

## Prompt 5 — turn the approved image into a 10-second cinematic ad

```text
Use the approved hero frame as the exact image-to-video source. Create a 10-second commercial with three model-native shots and native sound. Shot 1 (2 seconds): an immediate product-specific pattern interrupt with one controlled camera move. Shot 2 (5 seconds): escalate into a reveal or problem-to-dream transformation using foreground parallax, physically believable environmental motion and one clear visual payoff. Shot 3 (3 seconds): resolve on the exact product with a refined light sweep, clean negative space and a locked final hold for editor-added copy. Preserve the package, label, proportions, colors, materials and geometry in every shot. Do not morph or duplicate the product, invent claims or objects, change the label, create malformed anatomy, use chaotic motion or render text.
```

## Prompt 6 — final quality check

```text
Audit this ad output as a strict client creative director. Check product identity, label accuracy, composition, immediate benefit clarity, mobile readability, brand consistency, visual artifacts, invented claims, competitor elements and unreadable generated text. Give me: PASS or FIX, the three highest-risk problems, and exact correction instructions. Do not praise the result unless it is ready to show a paying client.
```

## Prompt 7 — script line to cinematic B-roll

```text
Create one continuous 5-second [VISUAL STYLE] B-roll shot that visually communicates: "[ONE SCRIPT LINE]". Treat the uploaded image as a strict character and wardrobe reference. Preserve the same face or blank head, clothing, colors, proportions and setting. Use one location, one subject action and one smooth motivated camera move. Begin with a readable close detail, reveal the meaning through controlled camera movement, and finish on a stable one-second hold. No scene changes, additional characters, captions, readable text, logos, interface, watermark, face generation, wardrobe changes, duplicate subjects, body morphing, extra limbs, flicker or camera shake. 16:9, premium commercial color grade, 24 fps.
```
