# Five-Second Cinematic Quality Gate

Do not call a generated clip client-ready until it passes every required row.

| Check | Pass condition | If it fails |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.6s hook | Visible motion or light change creates immediate curiosity without hiding the product | Regenerate with a simpler, clearer opening beat |
| One hero move | The camera performs one smooth, intentional move with believable depth | Remove secondary camera instructions and reduce movement |
| Signature payoff | One concept-specific moment happens around 3–4 seconds | Strengthen only the payoff cue; do not add more objects |
| Product identity | Package, label, color, geometry and number of products stay consistent | Reject the clip and regenerate with stronger preservation language |
| Human anatomy | Hands and face remain stable in Creator Style | Reject; simplify the action and reduce camera movement |
| Physics | Steam, liquid, fabric, dust, reflections and objects move naturally | Name only one environmental cue and remove the rest |
| Final hold | Last 0.8 seconds are stable and usable for editor-added copy | Ask for an explicit locked final hold |
| Artifacts | No flicker, melting, duplicated product, invented logo or generated text | Reject; do not try to hide a material identity error in editing |
| Mobile readability | Product and benefit remain obvious at phone size | Use a closer crop or cleaner Product Hero composition |

## Approval rule

- **9/9 pass:** usable for the demonstration.
- **7–8 pass:** one controlled regeneration.
- **6 or fewer:** change the source image or simplify the concept before spending again.

## Important limitation

A strong three-shot plan increases the chance of a cinematic result but cannot guarantee a perfect first generation. The final proof is one real 10-second API sample reviewed at actual size with sound. Add final copy and any additional mix or polish in the editor; the API result is the generated ad plate, not a guarantee of campaign performance.
