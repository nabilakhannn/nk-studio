# Trademark and identity policy

The source-code license does not grant permission to use the names **NK Studio** or **Nabila K**, associated logos, profile images, trade dress or other brand identifiers.

You may make factual statements such as “forked from NK Studio” and link to the official repository. You may not present a fork, paid service, course, download or account as official, endorsed or maintained by Nabila K without written permission.

Public forks intended for distribution should:

1. Rename the product and package.
2. Replace the logo, sample artwork and visual identity.
3. Remove claims that the fork is the official NK Studio.
4. Keep the AGPL attribution and license notices required by the code license.

The official source is <https://github.com/nabilakhannn/nk-studio>.
